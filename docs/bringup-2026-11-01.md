# Scale Controller Rev-A — Bring-up, 1 November 2026

First power-on of the PCBWay-assembled Rev-A boards (order YK1813827, 5 pcs).
Board #1 tested with `firmware/smoke-test.yaml`. Result: **works, after removing SW1/SW2**.

## Finding B1 — RESET and BOOT buttons short EN and GPIO9 to GND  (design error)

Symptom: power LED on, but the board never appears on USB. EN measured 0 V, D+ at U5 0 V.

Cause: the TL3305AF260QG has two internally joined leg pairs — the legs directly across
the body from each other (symbol pins 1–2 and 3–4; the symbol draws the bars). The
schematic wires pins 1 and 3 to GND and pins 2 and 4 to EN (SW2) / BOOT (SW1), so each
switch ties its signal to GND through the joined pair, pressed or not. Measured ~1 Ω
across the top-left and top-right legs of both switches, unpressed. ERC cannot see this
because the symbol presents four independent pins.

Effect: EN held low → chip permanently in reset. GPIO9 held low would also force download
mode on every boot.

Rev-A workaround (applied to board #1):

- **Remove SW1 and SW2.** Flashing and reset work over native USB without them. The
  physical tare is lost; use the Home Assistant "Tare" button. Or:
- **Keep the buttons**: cut or lift one diagonal pair of legs on each switch — top-left
  and bottom-right with USB-C at the bottom — so each internal pair keeps a single
  connection (GND on one side, EN/BOOT on the other).

Rev-B fix: wire GND to pins 1 + 2 and EN/BOOT to pins 3 + 4 (check against the TL3305
datasheet drawing before committing).

Board #1: one load-cell socket was damaged while desoldering the switches; repair pending.

## Socket pairs (correction)

From the board: **LC1 = J5, LC2 = J8 → S−; LC3 = J3, LC4 = J7 → S+.** A test needs one cell
in each pair — a single cell leaves the other input on its filter capacitor only. For the
most even corner response mount the S+ pair (LC3/LC4) diagonally. The 18 Sept schematic
review had "LC1/LC3 = J3/J7"; that was wrong and is corrected there.
