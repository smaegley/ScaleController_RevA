## What was fixed along the way (14–15 Sept)

Bugs found in the inherited design, all corrected in the schematic and reflected on the board:

- **Three separate 3.3V nets** (`3.3V`, `+3.3V`, `+3V3`) left the HX711 and the OLED
  unpowered. Unified onto one net.
- **HX711 input filters were wired in series** (S± → 100R → 4.7 nF → INA±), which
  blocks the DC bridge signal. Rewired as shunt caps to AGND.
- Stale KLP-5e leftovers removed: test points TP2/TP4, 104 silkscreen items,
  eight top-layer copper islands, duplicate ground zones, In2 power islands.
- Net classes: two classes had zero-annular-ring vias; `+5V` was in no class.
- Board had no mounting holes.

Layout decisions worth knowing:

- Load-cell connectors were re-ordered so the two S+ cells are adjacent
  (J3, J7) and the two S− cells adjacent (J5, J8). Wire the cells accordingly.
- HX711 cap/resistor column re-ordered pin by pin; NT2/NT3 net ties moved beside
  the connectors. AGND meets GND only at NT1.
- A five-lane bottom-layer bus runs along the top edge (between the antenna
  keepout and mounting hole H4) carrying the UART pair and GPIO8/18/19.
- USB D± use short bottom-layer hops around the connector's mirrored pin rows.
- J1 (USB-C) has a custom DRC rule allowing 0.127 mm between its own pads and a
  solid connection to the ground plane; J1 and U3 allow bridged mask apertures.

## Before ordering boards

1. **Silkscreen labels** — all board-level silk was purged. Needed: connector
   names and load-cell assignment (S+ → J3/J7, S− → J5/J8), BOOT/EN buttons,
   J6 pin names (3V3, GPIO8, GPIO18, GPIO19, GND), OLED header pins, board
   name and revision.
2. **Fab capability check** — J1's through-hole row has 0.35 mm hole-to-hole
   spacing; JLCPCB lists 0.5 mm. This footprint was built in the KLP-5e
   prototypes, but confirm with the chosen fab. Also confirm the 4-layer
   stackup (file has 0.1 mm prepregs; fabs will substitute their standard).
3. **Design rules** — `ScaleController_RevA.kicad_dru` has a global rule that
   silences the annular-ring check; narrow it to the ESP32 module's ground grid.
4. **Outputs** — schematic PDF, BOM with LCSC numbers, Gerbers + drill,
   pick-and-place. Then a quote.
